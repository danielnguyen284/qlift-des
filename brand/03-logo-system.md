# Logo System

## Brand facts

The QLIFT logo combines:

- a six-sided bolt/hexagon structure;
- a stylized letter Q;
- circular motion suggesting stable operation;
- a forward-leaning, bold QLIFT wordmark;
- a red Q as the wordmark accent and blue for the remaining letters.

## Approved configurations

1. Master vertical: symbol above wordmark.
2. Symbol only.
3. Master horizontal: symbol beside wordmark.
4. Master horizontal with slogan.

## Clear space

Minimum clear space around the logo equals **3/4 of the diameter/width of the circular form inside the symbol**. Keep this space free of text, imagery and other graphic elements.

## Minimum size

The PDF illustration associates the following values with each shown variant:

| Variant | Print | Digital |
|---|---:|---:|
| Master vertical | 20 mm | 120 px |
| Master horizontal | 47.5 mm | 300 px |
| Horizontal with slogan | 43 mm | 280 px |

Note: the body captions on the source page repeat “logo ngang” for all three items. The mapping above follows the left-to-right illustrations and must be confirmed when the original logo package arrives.

## Positive and reversed use

- Background tone at or below 40%: positive/standard logo.
- Background tone above 40%: reversed white logo.
- Black or white monochrome variants are fallback-only for media without color support.

## Placement

Approved positions:

- top-left;
- bottom-left;
- top-center;
- center;
- top-right;
- bottom-right.

Avoid center-left, center-right and bottom-center. If a format forces an exception, retain clear space and minimum size.

## App icon

- Use a rounded-square or circular container based on a 6x by 6x grid.
- Symbol occupies 3.5/6 of both width and height.
- Blue, white or gray versions may be selected to maintain contrast.
