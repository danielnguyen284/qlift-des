## Background roles

- Primary light: Ivory.
- Primary dark: Charcoal.
- Brand dark: QLIFT Blue.
- Supporting light: Mist Blue.

## Approved combinations

- QLIFT Blue + QLIFT Red.
- Ivory + QLIFT Blue.
- QLIFT Blue + Metallic Silver.
- Charcoal + Champagne.
- Ivory + Charcoal.
- Mist Blue + Charcoal.
- Architectural Beige + QLIFT Red.
- Architectural Beige + Teal.

## Avoid

- QLIFT Blue + Charcoal.
- QLIFT Blue + Teal.
- Metallic Silver + Mist Blue.
- Ivory + Architectural Beige.
- Metallic Silver + Architectural Beige.
- QLIFT Red + Teal.
