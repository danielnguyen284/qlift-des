# Typography

## Brand typefaces

- Primary: **Montserrat**.
- Secondary: **Gilroy**.
- Available weights shown for both: Light, Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold, Extra Bold and Black.

## Permitted systems

- Use one family consistently across a complete hierarchy; or
- use Montserrat for headings and Gilroy for supporting/body content.
- Do not alternate Montserrat and Gilroy between content blocks at the same hierarchy level.
- If Gilroy is used for headings, the guideline recommends continuing with Gilroy for body copy.

## Case and spacing

- ALL CAPS is acceptable for headings and short emphasis.
- Do not set long body copy in ALL CAPS.
- Maintain visible separation between headings and body copy.
- Recommended line-height range in the guideline: 120%-150% of font size.

## Print hierarchy in the guideline

| Role | Family / weight | Size | Color | Tracking |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| Headline 1 | Montserrat Bold or Gilroy Bold | 40 pt | `#262626` | 0 |
| Headline 2 | Montserrat Semibold or Gilroy Semibold | 30 pt | `#262626` | 0 |
| Paragraph 1 | Montserrat Regular or Gilroy Regular | 20 pt | `#262626` | 0 |
| Paragraph 2 | Montserrat Light or Gilroy Light | 12 pt | `#262626` | 0 |

## Licensing

Montserrat is available under an open font license. Gilroy is a commercial typeface; production usage requires client-provided licensed webfont files or written approval of a replacement. Until then, use Montserrat throughout as defined in `../tokens/brand-tokens.json`.
