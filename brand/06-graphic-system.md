# Graphic System

## Pattern

The QLIFT pattern is derived from a six-sided hexagonal structure. Repetition creates stable cube-like forms and a sense of continuous motion. The guideline connects it to technical durability, safety, precision, polished metal and modern premium quality.

Approved source examples use white pattern geometry on QLIFT Blue and QLIFT Red.

## Digital logo use

The guideline demonstrates:

- symbol in website browser UI;
- symbol as mobile app icon;
- symbol in a circular social avatar;
- logo layered over architectural elevator photography.

## Image frame

The shown frame system uses:

- a thin QLIFT Blue edge;
- a centered top logo tab/notch;
- a blue lower caption area;
- large white uppercase copy;
- a subtle hexagonal pattern in the lower area.

## Watermark

- Blend mode: Soft Light or Overlay.
- Opacity: 20%-40%.
- Keep primary image content unobscured.
