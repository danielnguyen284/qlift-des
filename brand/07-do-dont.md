# Brand Do / Don't

## Do

- Preserve approved logo proportions, clear space and minimum size.
- Use the standard logo on light backgrounds and white logo on dark backgrounds.
- Use one coherent typography hierarchy.
- Use approved color combinations.
- Keep body copy in readable sentence case.
- Use watermark at 20%-40% opacity with Soft Light or Overlay.
- Preserve the brand personality: professional, refined and approachable.

## Don't

- Do not swap logo colors.
- Do not rotate or flip the logo.
- Do not distort the logo.
- Do not turn the logo into an outline/stroke.
- Do not blur, add effects or apply a gradient.
- Do not crop the logo.
- Do not use an unapproved logo construction.
- Do not place the logo on a background with insufficient contrast.
- Do not mix equal-level Montserrat and Gilroy blocks arbitrarily.
- Do not use long ALL CAPS paragraphs.
- Do not use the prohibited color pairs in `04-color-system.md`.
