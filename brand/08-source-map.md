## Known ambiguities or source issues

1. **Tagline capitalization:** the tagline page reads “ưu Việt”; small artwork on the logo-variant page appears as “ưu việt”. This kit uses “ưu Việt” for editable copy and preserves the extracted artwork as-is.
2. **Minimum-size captions:** the source captions repeat “logo ngang” under all three images. This kit maps the values based on the actual illustrated variants: vertical 20 mm/120 px, horizontal 47.5 mm/300 px, slogan 43 mm/280 px.
3. **Font usage sentence:** one paragraph visually says both Montserrat and Gilroy can be used across the system, while the examples clearly enforce a consistent hierarchy. The normalized rule follows the examples and the later explanatory note.
4. **Frame description:** the text on the frame page repeats typography guidance and may be a template-copy error. The frame facts in this kit are limited to what is visually shown.
5. **Original assets unavailable:** temporary logo PNGs are high-resolution direct renders and pattern SVGs are vector extractions from the PDF. Neither is a substitute for client-approved master files.

## Review procedure

When original assets arrive, compare them with `../assets/logo/` and `../assets/patterns/`, replace temporary files atomically, update `../assets/README.md` and `../provenance/SOURCE.md`, then publish a reviewed system version.
