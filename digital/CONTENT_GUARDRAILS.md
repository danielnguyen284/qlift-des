## Protected factual content

- Logo/tagline usage and brand colors from the guideline.
- Technical specifications, load, shaft dimensions, PIT/OH, prices, installation claims, standards, warranty, service coverage, phone/email/address, and real-project claims must not be invented.

## Writing direction

- Write in clear, confident Vietnamese for homeowners. Explain technical decisions without sounding industrial or patronizing.
- Lead with safety, fit to the home, survey precision, transparent process, and after-sales continuity—not unsupported superlatives.
- Primary CTA: `Đăng ký tư vấn`. Secondary conversion: visible `Gọi QLIFT` action using the explicit placeholder `HOTLINE CHÍNH THỨC — BỔ SUNG TRƯỚC PRODUCTION` until a real number is supplied.
- Admin/CRM labels should be concise, operational, and status-led. Do not import competitor terminology when a plain QLIFT label works.
- Clearly label concept projects/images as `Hình ảnh định hướng` or equivalent in prototypes.

## Public copy budget

- Hero: headline maximum 7 Vietnamese words/2 lines; supporting text maximum 18 words; one primary CTA plus one compact call action.
- Do not place bullets, statistics, badges, trust statements, secondary paragraphs, or feature cards inside the hero.
- Standard homepage section: headline maximum 8 words; one supporting paragraph maximum 25 words; one visual or one action.
- Homepage copy should tease and route. Full explanations belong on dedicated pages or progressive-disclosure controls.
- “Complete website” means complete information architecture, not maximum text on every screen.

## Placeholder policy

- Clearly labeled placeholder copy is approved for design/prototype work.
- Never reuse Lorem Ipsum or demo contact details from the brand-office mockups in a production-facing design.
- Never convert TPEC competitor features into QLIFT claims without user confirmation.
